# Product matching and True Price

Matching is conservative. Barcode equality has the strongest confidence. Without a barcode, category and normalised title must match exactly. Cards additionally require compatible set/card number; graded items require grading company and grade; singles and graded variants require compatible condition. Similar-looking titles are not merged.

Unmatched offers remain independent. Legacy title-derived canonical IDs are provisional and do not authorize a merge.

True Price shows item price, delivery price, delivered total, collection and free-delivery information when present. An offer with unknown delivery is never labelled cheapest. “Lowest known delivered” means only the lowest total among offers whose delivery is known. Retailer stock and price can change after the last check.
